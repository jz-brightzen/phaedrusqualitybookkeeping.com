You already use Claude. You'd like to ask it about your business. How much cash will we have in March? Which customers are we losing money on? Why was October's margin down?

It should be easy. It isn't. Not yet.

The answer is only as good as two things: the data underneath, and the connection between that data and Claude. We take care of both. That's the CFO for your AI.

## Where it breaks today

We connect AI to real books in our own bookkeeping work. When Claude got a QuickBooks connector, we tried it right away. The connection errored out before the data loaded. That's normal. Here is where owners get stuck.

| Where it breaks | What it looks like | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Picking the connector** | Often there’s an official one, a community one, and a few half-built ones. Which is real? | We pick, install, and test the connectors for your systems. |
| **Keys and app registrations** | QuickBooks wants an Intuit app and OAuth. Stripe wants an API key. Bank data through Plaid needs production access Plaid has to approve. Payroll APIs are often partner-only. | We set up every credential and app, scoped as narrowly as the job allows. |
| **Tokens that expire** | It worked Monday. On Thursday it quietly stops. Nobody notices until the answer is wrong. | We monitor every connection and renew or repair it before you notice. |
| **Permissions** | Who should see payroll? Can the AI move money? Which Claude users get which data? | Read-only by default. Restricted keys. Access set per person. |
| **Messy books** | Uncategorized transactions, unreconciled accounts, duplicates. Claude answers fluently from bad data. | We clean and reconcile first, then run quality checks every week. |
| **Data in five places** | The model is in Google Sheets. Revenue is in Stripe. Costs are in QuickBooks. Headcount is in payroll. | We bring them into one private, finance-ready source with consistent definitions. |

None of this is hard for one afternoon. It's hard to keep working every week. That's the part we own.

## What we do

Two jobs.

**We organize your data.** QuickBooks, bank feeds, Stripe and other payments, payroll, and the spreadsheets your plan actually lives in. We reconcile them, apply your definitions of revenue, contribution, and customer acquisition cost, and keep them current. This is the same Data System and Accounting System behind every SDCFO engagement.

**We connect it to Claude.** We handle the keys, OAuth apps, connector setup, permissions, and security. Then we keep the connections alive. You open Claude and ask.

You don't learn what an API key is. You don't read Intuit's developer docs. You don't wonder whether the number is right.

## How it works

1. **Map.** A short call. We list your systems, who should see what, and the ten questions you most want answered.
2. **Clean.** We reconcile the books and fix what the quality checks find. Bank matches books. Nothing sits uncategorized. Balance-sheet accounts aren't stale.
3. **Connect.** We set up the apps, keys, and connectors, read-only by default, and link them to your Claude account.
4. **Ask.** We sit with you and run your ten questions. We check every answer against the source until you trust it.
5. **Keep it running.** We watch the connections, renew what expires, rerun the quality checks every week, and add systems as you grow.

## What you can ask Claude

Real questions, answered from your own books:

- How much cash will we have at the end of each of the next six months?
- Which ten customers produced the most gross profit this year? Which cost us money?
- Why did gross margin drop in October? Show me the accounts that moved.
- What did we spend on ads last quarter, and what did each new customer cost us?
- Who owes us money, and how late are they?
- Can we afford two more hires in Q1 without dropping below three months of cash?
- What changed in the books this week that I should know about?
- Draft the monthly update for my board from these numbers.

Ask Claude to show where each number came from: the report, the account, the date range. We set it up so it can.

## Trust and security

- **Read-only by default.** Claude can read your numbers. It can't pay a bill or move money.
- **Least privilege.** Restricted keys and narrow scopes. Each person sees only what they should.
- **Your accounts, your switch.** Connections run through accounts you own. You can revoke any of them yourself, any time.
- **Private by design.** Your data sits in a dedicated environment for your company, not a pooled template.
- **Your Claude plan.** You choose the plan. We walk you through its data terms before anything connects.
- **A person behind it.** A real finance team checks the books every week. AI answers, people check.

## What it isn't

It isn't a new app to learn. You keep using Claude.

It isn't a replacement for judgment. Claude can be wrong. Clean data and clear definitions make it wrong far less often, and the weekly review catches the rest. If you want a CFO in the room, that's the [CFO System](https://www.sdcfogroup.com/cfo-system).
