The Leucadia Club is a small nonprofit members' club on the coast in Encinitas. It has no finance department. Like most small clubs, it runs on volunteers, a point-of-sale system, a bank account, and a set of books someone has to keep straight.

John Zdanowski of San Diego CFO Group is the club's CFO. This is what we built.

## The problem

The books lived in QuickBooks Desktop, on one computer. Money came in through Square and moved through the bank. Nothing checked those three against each other.

The books had drifted from the last tax return. The chart of accounts had grown accounts nobody needed. There were errors to fix, and tax filings to catch up on.

The club also had no simple way to see its own members, book its space, or take a donation.

None of this is unusual. It's what happens when a small organization's finances are kept by whoever has time.

## What we built

**First, the foundation.**

- Moved the books from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online.
- Tied the books to the last tax return, so the opening numbers match what was filed.
- Cleaned up everything since that return.
- Prepared and filed the tax returns that were still open.
- Fixed the accounting errors and cleaned up the chart of accounts.

**Then, a custom AI that keeps it right.**

- It collects the data from Square, the bank, and QuickBooks and checks them against each other. When they disagree, we see it.
- It connects to Square and records the merchant-account fees in QuickBooks every month, automatically.

**Then, the tools the club runs on.**

- A member directory, built from the club roster.
- A reservation calendar.
- Donations inside the app.

## What changed

The books are in one place, online, and they match the tax return. The three sources of truth — Square, the bank, and QuickBooks — get checked against each other instead of drifting apart. The monthly Square fee entries now happen on their own.

And the club's board and members have one place to find each other, reserve the space, and give.

## Why it matters for your business

A members' club is a small business with the same problem every small business has: money moves through several systems, and the books are only as good as the last time someone reconciled them.

We fixed the foundation first, then built the AI on top of clean data. That order matters. An AI on messy books gives you fast, confident, wrong answers. An AI on clean, connected books gives you answers you can use.
