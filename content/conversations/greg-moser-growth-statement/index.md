John Zdanowski and Greg Moser, Friday Oct 2, 2026, about 4:07pm PT. Otter titled the recording "NO MEETINGS." About six minutes. Summary only.

They wanted the growth statement to become more than a flat row. Every business has an audience, turns some of that audience into a first payment, and tries to keep them coming back. Greg treated those as items in a row set, then added dimensions: a value proposition, and the channels you test it on.

The example was a 13-week cash forecast. Shipcom needs one. Weekly Accounting customers want one. QuickBooks does not make it easy. John said he would not build it the old way. He would build it with Greg, on Marvin, using data they already have, including a QuickBooks connection and the inventory data that makes a forecast hard.

They would test it as an open offer and with existing customers. Marvin OS founding members start the same way: a cheap wait-list test, then a machine that turns an idea into value on the income statement and, after that, into shareholders' equity. That is the door into Marvin's virtual economy. They stopped there.
