## Summary

The conversation centered on moving Marvin OS from a services-heavy implementation model to a productized platform. The aim is to use data from ShipCom onboarding transcripts and BrightZen partnerships to build a repeatable, scalable financial intelligence engine that replaces manual Google Sheets workflows.

## The Marvin OS financial engine

The current model puts implementation revenue first to sustain the team while building toward a recurring "App Store" model.

- **Immediate target:** $30,000 in monthly revenue from 10 high-value implementation clients (about $3,000 per project).
- **The 15% model:** Long-term recurring revenue comes from a 15% maintenance fee on products built in the Marvin ecosystem, similar to an App Store.
- **Self-liquidating value:** Every $100 spent on acquisition should return at least $100 right away, so growth can scale.

Pocket's initial-phase figures: implementation target 30,000; current platform revenue 10,000; average project fee 3,000.

## Productizing the 13-week cash forecast

The 13-week cash forecast moves from Google Sheets into a native Marvin OS view. That changes how financial health is communicated to entrepreneurs, not just where the numbers live.

- **Intranet instead of spreadsheets:** A live intranet view replaces static monthly reports, with a chat interface next to the data for context and goal tracking.
- **Integration spike:** The first big step is connecting QuickBooks and Stripe directly into Marvin OS for ShipCom, which serves as "Customer Zero."

## Financial quality matrix

Reporting is rated on four levels of data integrity and access:

- **Trouble:** No one is paying attention. High risk, no oversight.
- **Functional:** Someone is looking, but the data is messy, manual, and error-prone.
- **Integrated:** Click-through detail, linked analytics, and balance-sheet transparency.
- **World-class:** Seamless automation, a "taken care of" status. Inspired by *Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance*.

## Partnerships and scale

Distribution is the key to keeping AI services from becoming a commodity.

- **Channel strategy:** Work through partners such as ShipCom and BrightZen instead of taking direct clients. Partners bring both the budget and the customers.
- **The Sherpa role:** The team sits between the entrepreneur and the AI, removing friction so the entrepreneur can be "the most productive human being that's ever lived."
- **The "Truffle Pig" approach:** Find the easiest value propositions to sell and implement inside existing partner channels to build momentum.

## Operating metrics

Management here means giving an empowered team accurate, real-time data.

- **Sales visibility:** A dashboard of daily averages against actuals, showing how much is still needed to hit the daily goal.
- **Intentionality:** Focus strictly on revenue work (conversations and proposals) until the $30,000 MRR target is hit.
- **Data hierarchy:** Conversations lead to proposals, then signed agreements, then recurring revenue.

Data-to-revenue pipeline: onboarding transcripts become a common language, then a repeatable process, then a productized service, then 15% recurring revenue.
