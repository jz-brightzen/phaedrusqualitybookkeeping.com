## Summary

Marvin OS aims to automate the “90% of toil” that creates friction — moving from a system of record (data silos) to a **system of resolution** (goal-oriented orchestration). Central idea: dump fragmented software queues into one AI-managed **Bin**, so people spend time on judgment and creative work.

## Three pillars

1. **Data ownership** — the underlying DB (Salesforce, NetSuite, Shopify, …)
2. **Data UI** — screens and search
3. **Queues** — notifications, tickets, daily tasks

## Key points

- Queue fragmentation (Slack, Jira, email, ERP) drives stress and misaligned effort.
- Marvin surfaces where systems disagree (e.g. CRM sales vs bank invoices) and bubbles up truth.
- Framed as a “team for every IC” — delegate, solve, or close via an agent that knows org goals.
- Most SMEs lack documented goals/SOPs; Marvin should help structure emerge from work, not require a McKinsey-style intervention.
- Risk: helpful prioritization vs “nagging.” UX must enrich, not just hustle.
- Productivity is a red ocean; edge is org experience + proprietary data mapping (what “revenue” means across systems) so foundation models don’t Sherlock the thin layer.

## Roadmap phases (Pocket)

1. Data resolution across Salesforce / QuickBooks / Shopify  
2. Queue centralization — The Bin (Jira, Zendesk, email, …)  
3. Goal alignment — values and SMART goals in prioritization  
4. Autonomous execution — clear 30–90% of routine queues without humans
