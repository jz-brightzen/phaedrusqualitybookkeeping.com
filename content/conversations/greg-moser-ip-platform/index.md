John Zdanowski and Greg Moser, Friday Oct 2, 2026, about 4:18pm PT. Same Otter title, "NO MEETINGS." About eight minutes. This is the second half of the conversation. Summary only.

Greg's point: people worry AI will take their jobs, so he spent months asking where the value goes. Today intellectual property gets packaged by someone who funds it and owns the whole thing, while the people who built it get little. He thinks AI can change that. A person should be able to speak a framework they already use into a product and get paid for it.

Seeding that network is the hard part. Yelp worked because the restaurants were already on it. Greg would not start from an empty graph. Add the intellectual property that already exists, the SaaS products people use, and let people build apps on the tools they already run their work on. Approach the network from both sides: incumbents, and anyone who shows up with an idea.

John mapped it to Linden Lab. They gave people a body, tools to build a world, other people to build with, an audience, and a medium of exchange. Creators came because it was fun, then invited friends. Most people participate. A smaller group makes most of what gets made. The difference here is the real world, not a virtual one. A person can now embody an idea, test it in the market in days, and that has not been possible before. Shopify and Facebook did that for stores. Replit and the programmable tools do it for engineers. Marvin, John said, should do it for a poet. If a poet can monetize an experience, everybody is free to be one.

Greg said that was the point, the kind of thing that would excite Ian, and they should stop the recording.
