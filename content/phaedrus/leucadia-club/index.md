## Who we are

The Leucadia Club is a private, member-run social club on Coast Highway 101 in Leucadia, Encinitas. It was founded in 2013 to give its members a place close to home to relax, talk and get to know one another. More than a decade later, that is still the point.

We are about 55 members. Many of us live and work in North County San Diego. Some of us started our own companies. Others are partners in firms, lead teams at large organizations, or are earlier in their careers. Our members work in life sciences and healthcare, finance and investing, accounting, law and consulting, insurance, real estate, technology, consumer brands, and marketing and media. That mix is a big part of why people stay. You might come in for a drink and leave with a new friend, a good idea, or a straight answer from someone who has done the thing you are trying to do.

The clubhouse is our shared space. It has a members' bar with local beer and wine, room to work during the day, and a conference room for a quiet call or a small meeting. Members look after it together and keep it simple and comfortable.

We are a nonprofit social club, supported by our members' dues. We stay small on purpose so it feels like a neighborhood place, not a venue. New members join through a current member who sponsors them. Members may bring guests, as long as the guests stay with them.

If you know a member, ask them about the club. If you don't yet, we're happy to hear from you.
