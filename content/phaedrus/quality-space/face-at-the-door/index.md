I spent basically all of Saturday on club stuff.

It started like any other day. I got up and walked with Jake out back, showered, and went to dinner with Greg's family. Then Jake and Greg drove to LA. Greg showed Jake the LA warehouse and dropped him off in Santa Monica.

I went to the club. A guy from San Marcos I'd met on LinkedIn dropped off about $1,500 worth of hardware, and I spent the rest of the day playing with it.

By the end of it I had all the electronics it takes for a person to walk up to The Leucadia Club and be let in by face recognition.

The portal keeps a door log. It records every time the door opens for someone and every time it refuses someone. Only admins see it, and entries are deleted after 90 days. One of the first lines is mine:

> John Zdanowski (not a portal member) · Sat, Nov 21, 8:30 PM · Opened

Next to it is a fisheye photo of me standing at the door.

Grok Bot manages it. A member can be turned on or off within a minute. Claude wrote the code. GPT figured out what door hardware to buy, but it needs one specific measurement before I can be sure.

What's left is wiring. I'm going to run an ethernet cable through the ceiling and down to the door, to an access unit. That unit connects to the door strike through a low-voltage chain across the hinge. When a face is recognized, the strike opens.

Celia, Luke and Charlotte were the test cases for the whole process.

None of them were amused.

"And then I'd learn to code."
