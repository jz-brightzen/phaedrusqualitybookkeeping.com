## Rules carried into every page

Sister to WA **light/warm** (Golos, greens, peach pills) — **not** BrightZen-dark. Waitlist-first. Raise licensed trust. **Do not** copy parent “CPAs: Zero” swagger.

## 1. Homepage (top → bottom)

1. **Nav** — Weekly Accounting **Tax** lockup · How it works · Who it’s for · Trust · FAQ · **Join waitlist** (peach) · text link to weeklyaccounting.com  
2. **Hero** — Clean books. Tax AI. Senior CPA in the loop. Primary: **Join the January waitlist**. Trust chips: CPA in the loop · Separate insured company · Waitlist into January  
3. **Proof strip** — Built for the WA network (~400). Matt hears tax asks daily. No invented testimonials  
4. **How it works teaser** — Books → Zuni → CPA → file when ready  
5. **Who it’s for** — Existing WA first; inbound tax asks → same waitlist  
6. **Trust bar** — Will Haeberle (FL CPA) + associate + system · separate entity with own insurance (no overclaim before deal)  
7. **Waitlist embed** — Confirmation draft: no charge to hold the spot  
8. **CFO cross-sell** — Soft link to weeklyaccounting.com  
9. **FAQ teaser** → full FAQ  
10. **Footer** — Soft links · Privacy/Terms stubs (**ASK** legal)

**Mobile:** Waitlist CTA under hero; trust chips wrap; steps vertical; peach CTA full-width.

## 2. How it works

Books quality → tax system → CPA review → file when ready. Steps: books gate · Zuni (human-readable outcomes, not vendor price) · CPA loop · file when ready. Capacity note: January waitlist first. CTA: Join the January waitlist.

## 3. Waitlist form

**Fields (from waitlist GTM):** Company · Contact name · Email · Phone (optional ASK) · Source (CFO / Matt / other) · CFO name if applicable · States expected · Complexity · January consent · Notes.

**Do not collect:** Payment. Wholesale AI cost. Locked SKU choice as if purchase.

**Success (draft):** You’re on the waitlist. We’ll follow with timing and options. No charge to hold the spot.

## 4. Trust & team

Feel safe filing — senior CPA in the loop. Separate insured sister company. People shape: Will + associate + system. Won’t claim: anti-CPA swagger, fake seals, multi-year return-volume homepage promises. CTA: Join the January waitlist.

## 5. FAQ starters (all answers DRAFT)

1. What is Weekly Accounting Tax?  
2. Who is it for first?  
3. How does it work?  
4. What’s the January waitlist?  
5. How much does it cost? → **directional / finalizing only until John locks SKUs** — do not print locked dollars on the live tax site  
6. Same as my WA CFO?  
7. Who signs off?  
8. Separate insured company?  
9. Need perfect books?  
10. When can I file?

Omit from FAQ: wholesale AI cost, unconfirmed Will role, invented testimonials.

## 6. Cross-page mobile principles

Peach primary CTA full-width on small screens. Trust chips before price talk. No invented scarcity. High contrast. Soft parent link never louder than waitlist CTA.
