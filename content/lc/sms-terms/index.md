Last updated: October 11, 2026

**Program name:** Leucadia Club Member Alerts

**What you'll receive:** A text when another Club member @mentions you in a post in the Club's private member portal. Each text contains a short message and a link to that post.

**How to opt in:** Sign in to the member portal, open your My profile page, and turn on "Text me when someone @mentions me in a post," next to your own mobile number. Texts go only to members who turn this on. Consent is not a condition of membership or of any purchase.

**Message frequency:** Varies based on how often you are mentioned. Typically a few texts a month or fewer.

**Message and data rates may apply.** Check with your mobile carrier for details.

**To stop:** Reply **STOP** to any text, or turn the setting off on your My profile page. After you reply STOP, we will send one confirmation text and no more texts after that. To start again, turn the setting back on in the portal.

**For help:** Reply **HELP** to any text, or email accounting@leucadiaclub.com.

**Carriers:** Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Privacy:** See our [Privacy Policy](/lc/privacy-policy/). We do not share your mobile number or opt-in consent with third parties for marketing.

**Contact:** Leucadia Club, 828 N Coast Hwy Ste E, Encinitas, CA 92024. accounting@leucadiaclub.com
